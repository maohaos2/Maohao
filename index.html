About Me



I am a final-year PhD student at EECS@MIT supervised by Prof. Gregory Wornell. I obtained my bacholor of science degree in computer engineering at UIUC, where I was fortunate to be advised by Prof. Sanmi Koyejo and Prof. Yoram Bresler.

My research is driven by the goal of building more capable, reliable, and general-purpose artificial intelligence systems. I am particularly interested in three core challenges: improving reasoning and decision-making capabilities, ensuring safety and reliability, and enabling models to effectively acquire and process information across multiple modalities. My research focuses on:
  • Enhancing the reasoning capabilities of LLMs and AI agents.
  • Improving the reliability, safety, and trustworthiness of machine learning systems.
  • Developing multimodal LLMs, including vision-language and speech-language models.
